4. Model output can be wrong

Annotations, summaries, acronym expansions and companion notes are produced by a language model and by OCR. They can be inaccurate, incomplete or misleading, and OCR can misread text. fyrno7 checks that quoted passages exist in the document, but it cannot check that a judgement is correct. Verify anything important against the paper itself. The output is not academic, legal, medical or professional advice.
